# Refusal-control dataset (2026-08-10)

Control for the DA transfer results: same 394 scenarios as the Sonnet
scale-08 rung (165 train + shared 229 val), assistant turns regenerated by
Sonnet 5 under a categorical zero-justification refusal policy
(`prompts/difficult_advice/refusal/`, stages 7-9 only; spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-10-refusal-control-design.md`). If a student
trained on this matches the reasoned arms, the "why" in the training text is
not load-bearing; if it lands near base, the reasoning is doing the work.

## Dataset

- `data/difficult-advice/claude-sonnet-5-refusal/` — seeded by
  `code/difficult_advice/subset_scale08_prompts.py seed` (1:1 hard-fail match
  against the committed rung files), regenerated with
  `DIFFICULT_ADVICE_PROMPT_SET=refusal PIPELINE_MODEL=claude-sonnet-5
  PIPELINE_OUT_DIR=data/difficult-advice/claude-sonnet-5-refusal
  sample_prompts.py --responses-only` (without `PIPELINE_OUT_DIR` the run
  lands in `tmp/`), thinking ON (pipeline default), served
  over OpenRouter (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5`), 2026-08-10/11.
- Cost **~$33.2** (~$0.082/sample), in two passes: pass 1 halted at 100/394 on
  a cost overrun and pass 2 resumed from the checkpoint for the remaining 294
  after approval. The plan's ~$3–6 estimate was simply wrong — the driver is
  the ~13k-token constitution excerpt, re-sent uncached in all three
  (response/critique/rewrite) calls of every sample. Spending: `1b940aa`,
  `5d0b9e4`.
- Artifacts: `ft_dataset.jsonl` (394), `refusal-ft-qwen-nothink.jsonl` (394),
  train/val `refusal-scale-08.jsonl` (165) / `refusal-val.jsonl` (229).
- Train/val (system, user) turns are **byte-identical** to
  `data/difficult-advice/claude-sonnet-5-full-filtered/s5think-scale-08.jsonl`
  / `s5think-full-qwen-nothink-val.jsonl`; all 394 assistant turns differ
  (verified by the `split` matcher + explicit byte check).
- QC: `qc_report.md` — 211 keyword-flagged rows, dominated by false positives
  ("because" inside domain advice is fine; only justification *attached to a
  refusal* breaks the style contract). On human read: 1 clear leak (row 54),
  2 borderline (rows 149, 316) = **0.25–0.76%** against a 5% stop threshold;
  formatting deviation ~4.1% (16/394, mostly bullets in drafted deliverables
  plus inline bold) against the haiku45 20% failure benchmark; short tail has
  no stonewalls (the 27-char minimum is a correct bare refusal of an
  all-fraught request). **Verdict: passes, no regeneration needed.** The
  report's READ FIRST block is hand-written and a scanner re-run wipes it.
  `qc_review_sample.md` (20 transcripts) — **read before launching training.**
